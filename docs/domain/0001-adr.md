# DM-0001: ADR

## Status

accepted

## Definition

An architecture decision record: a dated, narrowly-scoped commitment with context, decision, and consequences. ADRs capture the why of the system.

## Avoid

- **design doc** — too broad, an ADR is one dated decision, not an evolving design overview
- **ticket** — tickets track work, ADRs record commitments
- **changelog entry** — changelogs describe what changed, ADRs explain why

## Relationships

An ADR records the decision a [SPEC](0003-spec.md)'s requirements depend on. The meaning of ADR itself is defined here, in the [Domain Model](0002-domain-model.md).
